# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-23)

First public release.

- Nightwing's escrima sticks in the Smoke Pellet's slot (key 6), with Nightwing's wheel and HUD icons.
- Aimed ricochet throw with lock-on markers: bounces through up to four thugs and returns to Batman's hand.
- Quickfire (double-tap 6) aims at the nearest thugs in front of Batman; one stick at a time.
- Hold 6 for one second to switch to Nightwing's Electromagnetic Blast and back, with an on-screen banner.
- Sticks carried crossed on Batman's back.
- Nightwing's animations are only added to Batman while the sticks are in use.
