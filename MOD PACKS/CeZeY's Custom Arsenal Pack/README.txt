========================================================================
 WHAT'S IN IT
========================================================================

RED HOOD'S DUAL HANDGUNS  (key 4, shares the REC gun's slot)
- Hold 4 for one second to swap the slot between the REC gun and the guns.
  A banner confirms it and the choice is remembered.
- Close waist-up aim camera with dead zone aiming: the reticle moves inside a zone
  and the camera follows it; shots go exactly where the reticle is.
- Every shot is lethal: shot thugs stay down, medics can't revive them and
  Detective Mode shows them as DECEASED.
- 12-round clip with an ammo counter, R to reload, quickfire by double-tapping 4.
- Pistols in thigh holsters; F5 keeps them in Batman's hands at all times.

THE ARKHAM KNIGHT'S SNIPER RIFLE  (key 7, shares the Disruptor's slot)
- The actual rifle the Arkham Knight uses in his boss fight, in Batman's hands
  and slung across his back.
- Hold 7 for one second to swap the slot between the Disruptor and the rifle.
- Red laser sight while aiming (the same laser the militia snipers use).
  F6 turns the laser on/off.
- 3-round magazine, R to reload, lethal shots with recoil, muzzle flash and tracer.
- Sniper kills scare the thugs who see them: they flinch, then run away for a
  while before they pull themselves together.
- Can break destructible objects and hit drones.

BATCLAW ZIP KICK  (Batclaw key, 2 by default)
- Double-tap the Batclaw key and hold the second tap while a thug is targeted:
  Batman fires the claw, flies at the thug and drop-kicks him.
- Normal Batclaw use is unchanged (single tap / hold as usual).
- Keyboard only for now.
